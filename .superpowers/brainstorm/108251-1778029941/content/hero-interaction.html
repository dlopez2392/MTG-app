User Interaction

Should the portal respond to touch/mouse?

A

Fully Interactive

Particles follow mouse/touch position. The vortex center shifts subtly toward the cursor. Scrolling creates a parallax depth effect. Touch drag on mobile swirls the particles. Feels alive and responsive.

B

Ambient Only

The portal swirls and particles drift autonomously — no user interaction. A beautiful, self-contained animation that plays on load. Simpler to implement, zero risk of janky touch handling. Lets the user focus on the content below.

C

Subtle Response

The vortex gently responds to device gyroscope (mobile) or mouse position (desktop) — a soft parallax shift, not direct particle control. Feels magical without being distracting. Particles remain autonomous but the whole scene has gentle depth movement.

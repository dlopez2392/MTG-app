Portal Color Palette

What colors should the vortex and particles use?

A: Brand Violet + Gold

Vortex in your accent violet (#7C5CFC) with gold/amber particles matching the title gradient. Cohesive with existing design tokens. Deep purple core fading to the dark background.

B: Blue Mana Storm

Electric blue core shifting to violet edges. Cyan and lavender particles. More "spell-casting" energy feel. Cooler tones contrast nicely with the warm gold title.

C: Golden Rift

Warm amber/gold core transitioning to violet at the edges. Ember-like golden particles with occasional violet sparks. Feels like a planeswalker spark igniting. Dramatic and warm.

D: Multi-Chromatic

Cycles through multiple colors — gold core, violet mid, blue outer edges — with particles in all three tones. The most visually complex. Feels like all five colors of mana converging.

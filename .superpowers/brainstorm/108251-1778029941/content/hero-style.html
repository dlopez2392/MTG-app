Hero Banner Redesign — Visual Direction

What kind of 3D + particle effect vibe fits MTG Houdini?

A

Mystic Portal

A swirling 3D vortex/portal behind the title with floating mana particles drifting outward. Think magical energy converging — glowing orbs in violet/gold tones spiraling slowly. The title text has depth with a subtle 3D perspective tilt.

B

Dust & Sparks Dissolution

Building on the existing "Houdini dust" animation — the title materializes from a cloud of glowing particles that scatter and reform. Ember-like sparks drift upward continuously. More of an evolution of what you already have, but much more dramatic with real particle physics.

C

Floating Card Universe

MTG card backs (or art crops) floating in 3D space behind the title at various depths with parallax. Subtle particle dust connecting them like constellation lines. The cards slowly rotate and drift, creating depth. Title sits in the foreground with a glass/frosted effect.

D

Mana Constellation

The five MTG mana symbols (W/U/B/R/G) rendered as glowing 3D orbs orbiting slowly around the title like a solar system. Particle trails connect them. Star-field background with subtle depth parallax on mouse/scroll movement. Clean and iconic.
